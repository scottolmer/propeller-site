# Approved concept

## Working title

How to Research NFL Player Props: A Game-Week Workflow

## Viewer and search intent

- Primary question: How do you research NFL player props?
- Audience: NFL fans and DFS or pick'em users who want a repeatable research process rather than a list of unsupported picks.
- Search intent: Educational, with a product-assisted walkthrough.
- Single learning outcome: The viewer can verify a current NFL prop, evaluate role, injury, matchup, market, and model context, then decide whether the evidence is sufficient to continue or whether the correct decision is no action.

## Production shape

- Target duration: 5 to 7 minutes.
- Format: 1920x1080 desktop-and-mobile product walkthrough with restrained editorial overlays.
- Narration: Local Kokoro stock voice `am_michael` after script approval.
- CTA: Read the NFL research guide, then open the NFL analyzer when a verified slate is available.
- Freshness rule: Show the current public slate state at capture time. If no current NFL prop exists, use only clearly labeled historical NFL examples for the line, confidence, and agent-breakdown walkthrough.

## Guardrails

- Confidence is directional signal strength, not a win probability or guarantee.
- A line move does not establish why the market moved or prove that either side is favorable.
- Propeller is a research and analytics platform, not a sportsbook.
- No stale or historical NFL row may be presented as current.
