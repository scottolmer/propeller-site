# Approved concept

## Working title

How to Use a Free NFL Player Prop Analyzer: Step-by-Step Walkthrough

## Strategic role

- Primary question: What is a free NFL player prop analyzer, and how do you use one correctly?
- Primary target: `free NFL player prop analyzer`
- Search intent: Product-led tutorial with a clear educational answer.
- Canonical destination: `https://propellerpicks.com/analyzer/?sport=nfl`
- Supporting destination: `https://propellerpicks.com/picks/nfl/`
- Audience: NFL DFS and pick'em researchers preparing for the 2026 season, especially new Propeller users.
- Single learning outcome: A viewer can open the NFL tab, verify whether a current slate exists, locate the player/stat/line, read direction before confidence, understand the free analyzer's limits, and know when to stop rather than force a conclusion.
- CTA: Open the free NFL Analyzer, then use the NFL research guide or full workspace when more context is needed.
- Target duration: about 5 minutes.

## Current-state truth

Verified July 31, 2026: the public NFL slate endpoint reports `offseason`, season 2026, week 0, zero current props, and the message “NFL regular-season props return when the season opens.” The video must show that state honestly.

To demonstrate the interface while the live NFL tab is empty, the walkthrough may use the already sourced Josh Allen archived 2025 Week 17 passing-yards fixture. Every frame containing that record must carry a persistent disclosure that it is a historical interface example, not a live line or published pregame recommendation.

## Required visual coverage

- Current free Analyzer landing page on desktop and mobile.
- NFL tab selection and current zero-slate state on desktop and mobile.
- Search/player selection behavior explained with a labeled historical interface example.
- Exact player, stat, line, direction, and confidence locations on desktop and mobile.
- Clear separation between the free analyzer output and the deeper signed-in research workspace.
- Methodology, results, and NFL research-guide links as the final verification/next-step sequence.

## Guardrails

- Do not call confidence a win probability.
- Do not promise accuracy, profit, an edge, or an outcome.
- Do not imply that training camp creates current regular-season player-prop lines.
- Do not present historical data as current.
- Do not imply the free analyzer is an optimizer or that it submits DFS entries.
- Propeller is an independent research and analytics platform, not a sportsbook.
