## Title

How to Use a Free NFL Player Prop Analyzer (Step-by-Step)

## Description

Learn how to use a free NFL player prop analyzer step by step: verify the current slate, confirm the exact player-stat line, read direction before confidence, and recheck fresh NFL information before making your own decision.

Open Propeller's free NFL Analyzer: https://propellerpicks.com/analyzer/?sport=nfl

In this walkthrough, the live NFL tab is shown in its verified offseason state with no current props. A persistently labeled historical Josh Allen interface example demonstrates the in-season workflow without presenting an old line as current.

You will learn how to:

- Check whether a current NFL slate exists
- Confirm the player, stat, exact line, and platform context
- Read an OVER or UNDER direction before the confidence score
- Understand why confidence is not win probability
- Send a structured question to Propeller's AI betting prompt builder
- Recheck player status, expected role, line movement, and source freshness
- Recognize when “no action” is the right result

Propeller is an independent research and analytics platform, not a sportsbook. It does not place entries, and no model signal guarantees an outcome.
