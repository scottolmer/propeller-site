**Search and answer-engine intent:** `free player prop analyzer`, `AI player prop analyzer`, and `how do you research player props`.

**Single learning outcome:** A viewer can confirm the exact current line, understand the displayed direction and confidence, inspect supporting and conflicting agents, recheck freshness, and either continue their research or pass.

**Target runtime:** 4 minutes 30 seconds to 5 minutes.

**CTA:** Explore the current Prop Analyzer at `https://propellerpicks.com/analyzer/`.

**Required coverage:** Use the same current or clearly labelled illustrative prop in desktop and mobile. Show the exact locations of the line, direction, confidence, agent breakdown, and freshness/context check. No unrelated UI may appear under narration.

**Freshness and compliance:** Capture the interface near render time; label historical or illustrative examples. Confidence is directional signal strength, not a calibrated win probability or guarantee. Propeller is a research and analytics platform, not a sportsbook.
