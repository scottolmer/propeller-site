# How to Use Propeller's Free AI Player Prop Analyzer

Final script draft for approval
Target: approximately 4:00–4:30, landscape product walkthrough

## Viewer answer

Use the analyzer in this order: confirm the exact player prop and current line; read the displayed direction and confidence without treating confidence as probability; inspect the supporting and conflicting agents; recheck freshness, news, and line context; then continue research or pass.

## Narration

See [`final.txt`](final.txt) for the text-to-speech-ready narration. It is intentionally written without screen-specific names, numbers, or player claims so the final capture can use a current prop and keep desktop and mobile visually synchronized.

## Factual source notes

- `guides/how-to-analyze-player-props/index.html`: The public analyzer uses sport-specific signals; available inputs can include matchup, injury, pace, usage, no-vig market context, hit-rate trends, minutes, and rest. It presents a directional 50–100 confidence score.
- `help/how-do-confidence-scores-work/index.html`: Higher scores indicate stronger support for the shown side; the score is not a win probability or guarantee.
- `guides/how-ai-sports-betting-works/index.html`: A changed line is a different research question; late scratches, source outages, role changes, and missing data can degrade analysis.

## Capture constraints
