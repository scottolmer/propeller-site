The hardest part of a player prop is not finding an opinion. It is knowing what supports it, what conflicts with it, and when to pass.

That is why we built Propeller Picks: an AI-assisted research workspace for player props, DFS and pick'em research, and fantasy projections—not a sportsbook.

Start in the free Prop Analyzer. Choose a sport, find a player and market, then open the research behind the number. On desktop or mobile, confirm the line, direction, and freshness.

Next is confidence. Propeller shows a directional 50-to-100 confidence score: how strongly the available signals support the displayed More or Less direction. It is not a win probability, and it is never a guarantee.

The difference is that you can inspect the reasoning. Available sport-specific signals surface their agreement and conflict—from market context and recent usage to injuries, matchup, environment, and lineup context. Signal availability and weighting change by sport, prop, and model calibration, so this is not a black box with one fixed agent count.

For MLB hitters, Propeller Fantasy adds a DraftKings-scoring view with a floor, point projection, and ceiling.

We also keep performance language honest. Propeller publishes a documented historical archive and clear methodology, but we do not present that archive as a generic forward-tested win rate or a promise of profit.
