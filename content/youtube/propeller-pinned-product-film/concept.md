# Propeller Picks: The Research Layer for Player Props

## Approved concept

A two-minute, 16:9 flagship introduction for the pinned Propeller Picks YouTube
channel. It is founder-led in message but does not use an on-camera founder or a
synthetic founder avatar. A composed stock narrator carries the story over real,
current or clearly labelled historical product footage, with restrained editorial
motion and proof cards.

## Viewer question

What is Propeller Picks, how is its product different from a typical picks
service, and what can a new user actually inspect before acting on a model
signal?

## Audience and intent

Prospective users, channel visitors, search viewers, and prospective partners
who need a concise, credible explanation of the platform and its technical
approach.

## Single learning outcome

The viewer can explain that Propeller is an independent research workspace—not a
sportsbook or a promise machine—and knows to examine the line, directional
confidence, available sport-specific signals, and context before making a
decision.

## CTA

Start with the free Prop Analyzer at https://propellerpicks.com/analyzer/.

## Production requirements

- Target 118–125 seconds; 1920x1080 at 30fps.
- Use stock Kokoro narration (`am_michael`), never an imitation of the founder.
- Include paired desktop and mobile product coverage for line, confidence, and
  agent breakdown; include desktop fantasy-projection coverage.
- Use current GA4/GSC aggregates only as dated traction cards, not as user or
  revenue claims.
- Do not claim a generic historical win rate. The public archive is documented
  data, not a uniquely published forward-tested ROI or profitability record.
- The investor-style framing is product and traction oriented; it contains no
  fundraising ask.
