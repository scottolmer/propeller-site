# Approved concept

## Title

How to Use the PrizePicks Research Page: From Cheat Sheet to Line Check

## Viewer question

How do I move through the Propeller PrizePicks research page on desktop and mobile, inspect the exact row and evidence, and verify whether the same projection is actually available in PrizePicks?

## Audience and intent

Product-led workflow for new Propeller users. The focus is the page-to-line-check sequence, not general analysis theory, prompt building, payout rules, or entry construction.

## Learning outcome

The viewer can find current research, match the player, stat, and line, read direction and confidence, inspect dated agent evidence, confirm freshness, and stop when the actual PrizePicks projection cannot be verified.

## CTA

Use `/picks/prizepicks/` for the current public preview and keep the permanent cheat-sheet explainer beside it at `/guides/prizepicks-cheat-sheet/`.

## Production boundary

The live platform-backed PrizePicks line endpoint returned no records on August 17, 2026. The walkthrough therefore demonstrates the correct no-match result instead of inventing an actual PrizePicks projection.
