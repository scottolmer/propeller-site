How to Compare Player Prop Odds Quickly (Desktop Workflow)

## Description

Compare player prop odds by matching the exact same question first: player, stat, direction, line, source, and timestamp. Then keep sportsbook price separate from pick'em or DFS payout terms, add Propeller's signal and evidence, refresh every source, and choose no action when the rows do not match.

This desktop workflow uses a simple manual ledger and current Propeller Analyzer and Pick6 calculator screens. Propeller does not automatically compare every sportsbook, DFS product, or pick'em platform. The examples in the workflow are illustrative, not live lines or recommendations.
