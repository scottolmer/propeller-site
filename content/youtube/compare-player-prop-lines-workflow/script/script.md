# How to Compare Player Prop Odds Quickly: A Desktop Research Workflow

Status: final narration approved under the user's autonomous Wave A batch authorization.

Target: about 4 minutes, landscape broadcast-editorial product walkthrough.

## Viewer answer

Compare only matching rows: player, stat, direction, exact line, source, and timestamp. Keep sportsbook price separate from pick'em or DFS payout terms. Add Propeller's direction, confidence, and supporting evidence only after confirming its displayed line. Refresh before deciding, and return no action when the lines, timing, terms, or evidence do not match.

## Truth boundaries

- Propeller does not automatically compare every sportsbook, DFS product, or pick'em platform.
- Propeller confidence is directional signal strength for the displayed line, not a calibrated probability or guarantee.
- A different line is a different research question.
- Sportsbook prices and pick'em/DFS multipliers or payout terms are different types of information and are not compared as one universal number.
- Platform rules, grading, promotions, limits, multipliers, and payouts can change. The live platform terms and final screen control.
- No-action is an explicit, valid research outcome.
- Propeller is a research and analytics platform, not a sportsbook, and does not place wagers or submit entries.

## Production treatment

The visual spine is a desktop research ledger that fills in as the narration advances. Fresh Propeller Analyzer and Pick6 calculator captures appear inside restrained browser and phone frames. The line-comparison examples are conspicuously labelled `ILLUSTRATIVE WORKFLOW · NOT LIVE LINES`; fictional platform names prevent accidental current-price claims. The design uses the Propeller paper/orange/ink palette, Familjen Grotesk, IBM Plex Sans, and IBM Plex Mono with precise callouts and continuous, subtle camera motion.
