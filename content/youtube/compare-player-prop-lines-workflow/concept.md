# How to Compare Player Prop Odds Quickly

## Approved concept

How to Compare Player Prop Odds Quickly: A Desktop Research Workflow

## Locked brief

- Primary question: How can someone compare the same player prop across multiple platforms without confusing the line, price, payout terms, or research signal?
- Audience: A desktop-first DFS, pick'em, or sportsbook researcher who wants a repeatable comparison process in plain language.
- Learning outcome: The viewer can build a manual comparison ledger, match the exact player/stat/direction/line, record source and timestamp, separate price from payout context, add Propeller's signal and evidence, and accept no action when the rows do not match or the evidence is stale.
- CTA: Open the current Propeller Analyzer at `https://propellerpicks.com/analyzer/`. The planned companion page `/guides/compare-player-prop-lines/` is intentionally not linked until it exists.
- Target duration: About 4 minutes.
- Truth constraint: Propeller does not automatically compare every sportsbook or pick'em platform. It is one research-and-evidence layer inside a manual workflow.
- Review policy: Produce a 1280x720 review cut only. A 1080p master, upload, and publication require later approval.
