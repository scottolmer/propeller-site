Underdog payout math starts with one simple formula: your entry amount multiplied by the multiplier shown for that entry. There is no single fixed payout for every card. The format, number of picks, selection modifiers, promotions, and even your location can change what you see.

In this walkthrough, I will show you how to estimate a Standard payout, read a Flex result, and spot the number that matters before you submit anything. This is research and arithmetic, not a prediction or a guaranteed return.

Start with the free Underdog payout calculator on Propeller Picks. Choose Standard or Flex, select the number of picks, and enter an amount. The calculator uses published base multipliers to show a scenario. Underdog's final entry screen is always the source of truth.

Here is a Standard example. Choose five picks and a ten-dollar entry. The current published base multiplier for a five-pick Standard entry is twenty times. Ten dollars multiplied by twenty gives a two-hundred-dollar total payout if every active pick is correct. That total includes the original entry amount. The calculator separates payout from payout minus entry so you do not confuse the two.

The hit-rate field is different. It lets you test your own per-pick assumption. It is not Propeller's forecast for a specific card, and it does not prove the selections are independent. Treat it as a what-if control, not a recommendation.

Now switch to Flex. Flex is available for three through eight picks. In this six-pick, ten-dollar example, the current base table shows twenty-five times if all six are correct. It also lists smaller amounts after one or two losses: two-point-six times for five of six, and zero-point-two-five times for four of six.

Those lower Flex numbers are up-to amounts. Which selection loses can matter because a selection's modifier may no longer apply. Underdog also says alternative projections, correlated picks, promotions, and other entry details can raise or lower the displayed multiplier. In Champions states, prize-pool results can make the final amount vary as well.

So use a four-step check. First, confirm whether you are seeing Classic or Champions where you are physically located. Second, match Standard or Flex and the exact pick count. Third, multiply the amount by the displayed multiplier. Fourth, compare the calculator result with Underdog's current entry screen before submitting.

If the two amounts differ, trust the current Underdog disclosure, not an old chart or screenshot. Pick'em is not offered everywhere, and eligibility can change.
