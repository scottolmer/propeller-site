# Storyboard and shot list

## Revised product-led cut

The approved narration is unchanged. The visual edit now behaves as a new-user walkthrough:

1. Mobile confidence close-up: confidence is read beside direction.
2. Mobile Picks board: choose a sport, scan ranked props, open a pick.
3. Mobile Slate Snapshot: orient to the slate and shortlist candidates.
4. Desktop Props Board: sort and open the historical example.
5. Desktop prop header: confirm player, direction, line, and market snapshot.
6. Desktop confidence close-up: zoom from the full context into the confidence tile.
7. Mobile Pick Detail: confirm the line and review the game log.
8. Mobile View Controls: sort, filter by market, direction, and line source.
9. Desktop Why This Lean and Model Breakdown: inspect evidence and agreement.
10. Public methodology page: explain raw direction versus normalized strength.
11. Mobile Model Read: separate confidence, hit-chance estimate, and signal mix.
12. Desktop market comparison: compare the model view with implied probability.
13. Mobile search and final scan-open-verify-decide workflow.

## Screen-capture list

1. Propeller analyzer landing state.
2. A prop card showing direction, line, confidence, and timestamp.
3. The underlying signal or reasoning view.
4. A line/platform comparison if one is available without exposing personal data.
5. The methodology or confidence-score help page.

## Motion and typography

- Canvas: 1920×1080, 16:9.
- Safe area: keep primary text inside the center 80%.
- Background: near-black navy with faint analytical grid.
- Accent: Propeller green for confidence and orange only for cautions.
- Type: bold geometric sans for headlines; monospaced numerals for scores.
- Pace: change the visual every 4–7 seconds without constant camera motion.
- Captions: two lines maximum; highlight one key phrase per sentence.

## Accuracy guardrails

- Always display the recommendation direction beside confidence.
- Never label confidence as predicted probability or expected hit rate.
- Do not use “lock,” “guaranteed,” or “sure thing.”
- If a real prop is shown, capture its line and timestamp together.
- Keep historical archive claims separate from the prospective record.
