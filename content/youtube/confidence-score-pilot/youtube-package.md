## Description

What does a 72% Propeller confidence score mean? It measures the strength of the model’s directional signal for the side shown—not a guaranteed 72% chance of winning.

In this video, we explain the difference between direction, confidence, and implied probability; the signals that can influence a score; and the four checks to make before using any player-prop analysis.

## Companion-page answer block

Propeller confidence measures the strength of the model’s directional signal for the recommended over or under. A 72% confidence score does not mean a guaranteed 72% probability of winning. Users should verify the current line, timestamp, supporting signals, market context, and payout before making their own decision.
