# Approved concept

## Title

What Is in a PrizePicks Cheat Sheet?

## Viewer question

What information should a trustworthy PrizePicks cheat sheet contain, how should each field be read, and what can it not prove?

## Audience and intent

Evergreen, answer-engine-focused explainer for casual PrizePicks and pick'em researchers who want a plain-language checklist rather than a pick promise.

## Learning outcome

The viewer can separate the player, stat, exact line, freshness, supporting evidence, direction, confidence, and limitations, then perform an independent platform check before using the research.

## CTA

Read the permanent companion answer at `/guides/prizepicks-cheat-sheet/` and use `/picks/prizepicks/` only for currently available Propeller research.

## Production boundary

The August 17, 2026 Christian Franklin row is current Propeller analysis, not a verified PrizePicks-native projection. No platform-backed PrizePicks line was available at capture time, so the example ends with a clearly labeled failed line check.
