## Description

A trustworthy PrizePicks cheat sheet should show the exact player, stat, line, source-analysis time, supporting evidence, directional read, confidence context, missing inputs, and limitations. This evergreen explainer shows how those fields fit together—and why the current PrizePicks projection still has to be verified.

Model confidence describes how strongly the available signals support the displayed direction for the exact line. It is not a calibrated win probability, payout estimate, recommendation, or guarantee. Lines, availability, rules, and player status can change. Verify the current PrizePicks board and terms before making your own decision.
