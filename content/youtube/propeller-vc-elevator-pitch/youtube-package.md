## Proposed title

Propeller Picks: The Research Layer for Player Props & Fantasy

## Proposed description

Propeller Picks is an AI-assisted research platform for player props and MLB
fantasy research. This overview shows how the free Prop Analyzer connects line
context, available sport-specific signals, and inspectable reasoning across web
and mobile.

Rather than asking people to trust a black-box pick, Propeller is built around a
repeatable workflow: discover the market, inspect the evidence, and use
confidence as directional signal strength—not a win probability or guarantee.

Explore the free Prop Analyzer: https://propellerpicks.com/analyzer/
