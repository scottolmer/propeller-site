# Propeller Picks: Investor Elevator Pitch

## Approved concept

A two-minute, 16:9 investor-oriented companion cut. It is founder-led in message
but uses no on-camera founder, no synthetic avatar, no user-count claim, no
revenue claim, no generic historical win-rate claim, and no fundraising ask.

## Audience and outcome

Prospective investors and strategic partners should understand the fragmented
research problem, the inspectable product experience, the technical product
principle, initial organic-discovery proof, and the expansion path across
player-prop and fantasy research.

## Visual direction

Make the product the protagonist. Use desktop/mobile device pairings, concrete
signal-detail crops, restrained data-room style evidence cards, and a premium
editorial visual system. This is a product pitch, not a slide deck, a betting
commercial, or an avatar presentation.
