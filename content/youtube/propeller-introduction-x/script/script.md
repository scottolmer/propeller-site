# Propeller Picks: What It Is — X/Twitter Intro

**Format:** 9:16 vertical · target 55–60 seconds · stock Kokoro voice `am_michael`

## Narrative intent

Lead with the category problem, then establish three differentiators: free research access, inspectable evidence, and public proof with visible limitations. The phrase “historical win rate” is intentionally not used as a headline because the public archive contains repeated snapshots and legacy retrospective rows; it is not a uniquely published forward-test or ROI record.

## Claims and sources

- Propeller is a free AI player-prop analyzer for DFS and pick'em research, supports current lines, and never places entries or wagers: [Analyzer](https://propellerpicks.com/analyzer/).
- It evaluates player, market, line, game context, team environment, recent usage, plus separate agents such as matchup, usage, market, form, injury context, and game script: [Methodology](https://propellerpicks.com/how-it-works/).
- Current public Results page, checked July 22, 2026: 2,185,007 raw historical analysis rows and 293,020 collapsed public-ledger entries. The same page says these are research records, not a clean cohort of uniquely published recommendations or a defensible ROI claim: [Results](https://propellerpicks.com/results/).

## Required on-screen qualifier

During the archive sentence, show: `Historical archive • descriptive outcomes • not forward-tested ROI • checked Jul 22, 2026`.
