# Approved concept

A concise, 9:16 social introduction for X/Twitter: what Propeller Picks is, how its AI player-prop research works, what makes the product different, and how to read its public historical record responsibly.

## Editorial angle

"No blind picks. Inspect the research." The video should feel like a confident product introduction, not a gambling-results ad. It will show the public historical archive as proof people can inspect, while explicitly stating that the archive is descriptive historical context rather than a forward-tested ROI claim.
