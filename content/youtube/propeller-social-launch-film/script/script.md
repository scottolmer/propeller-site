# Narration and production notes

## Voice

- Stock narration: local Kokoro `am_michael`.
- Delivery: energetic, confident, direct; no salesy urgency and no betting-tip cadence.
- Target read: roughly 70–78 seconds after natural pauses.

## Claim guardrails

- “Confidence” is a 50–100 directional model-confidence score, not a calibrated probability or guarantee.
- No profitability, return, win-rate, ROI, “lock,” “edge,” or fixed agent-count claims.
- The product is an independent research workspace; it does not take wagers, place wagers, or submit entries.
- Fantasy statement is deliberately scoped to current MLB hitter projections: floor, central projection, ceiling.

## Social edit language

- Build in 9:16 with large permanent captions and a safe lower-third area.
- Product screens are the proof. Every line that names line, confidence, breakdown, or fantasy must land on the exact matching desktop and mobile crop.
- Use editorial typography and kinetic transitions between UI moments; do not use a generic slide deck or stock gambling footage.
- Any previously captured UI must carry a small but legible “Illustrative product capture · July 2026” label.
