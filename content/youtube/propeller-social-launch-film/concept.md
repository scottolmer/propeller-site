# Propeller Picks: The Better Starting Point

A 75-second, 9:16 social launch film for Reels, TikTok, and Shorts. This is Propeller's public coming-out story: an energetic, credible answer to who Propeller is, what it is, where it lives, when it is useful, and how it helps fans research DFS, pick'em, and MLB fantasy decisions.

The creative spine is not a product tutorial. It is a quick, high-contrast product film: real desktop and mobile moments, sharp editorial typography, sports-energy transitions, and a single clear promise—less noise, more context, and a more enjoyable way to research the games people already follow.

The film ends at the free public Prop Analyzer. It does not promise profits, state a win rate or ROI, claim a fixed number of agents, or imply that confidence is a probability or guarantee.
