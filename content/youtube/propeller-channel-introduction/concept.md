# Approved concept

The definitive pinned YouTube introduction to Propeller Picks: an in-depth walkthrough of the free Analyzer and full research workspace, confidence and agent evidence, desktop and mobile flow, public historical record, methodology, limitations, and responsible use.

## Positioning

This is the channel’s "Start Here" video—not a picks commercial. It should answer the questions a new user has before signing up: What is Propeller? What can I actually do with it? Why should I trust the process? What do the score and record mean? What does the product not promise?

## Format

16:9, 4–5 minutes, real product walkthrough as the visual spine. The video can be clipped later for X/Twitter, but this master is designed for YouTube channel placement and search.
