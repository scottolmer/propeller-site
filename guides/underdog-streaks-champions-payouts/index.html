How Underdog Streaks and Champions payouts work

Streaks describes how an entry progresses through correct picks. Champions describes a settlement basis that can apply to a winning entry, including a Streak. Read those as two separate questions: what format is the entry, and how will a winner be paid?

This guide summarizes Underdog’s help-center articles checked September 9, 2026. Platform terms, eligibility, and entry screens can change; Underdog’s current rules control.

The direct answer

Streaks and Champions can operate together. A Streak concerns progress toward 11 correct picks. Champions concerns the points and pool share used to settle a winning entry. Classic Standard/Flex is another format with its own base-table calculator; that calculator does not calculate Streak progress or Champions pool shares.

How Underdog Streaks work

Underdog’s Streaks Rules says a Streak begins with two picks; after both are correct, the next pick becomes available, continuing toward 11 correct picks. Its published base-prize wording and any modifiers are specific to Streaks, rather than a rate that applies across Underdog formats.

The same rules say later Streak picks continue the entry, void treatment differs between the first two picks and later picks, and Win Now can close a Streak. In a Champions contest, Win Now locks Champions Points, connecting the Streak’s progress with its Champions settlement.

How Champions Points and payouts work

Underdog’s Champions Points article says points are entry fee multiplied by a difficulty multiplier; selection count, selection likelihood, and entry fee affect the points. A winning entry’s payout is its points divided by total points earned by winners, applied to the Champions prize.

Its Payout Differences and Discrepancies article says Champions is peer-to-peer, so more winning points can lower the dollar value of each point. The displayed average prize is an estimate based on past win rates, not a fixed result.

Illustrative Champions calculation

Same hypothetical entry, different winning-point totals

Invented assumptions for arithmetic only: a winning entry has 20 Champions Points and the cash prize pool is $1,000. These are not live contest terms, a forecast, or an Underdog example.

10,000 total winning points: 20 ÷ 10,000 × $1,000 = $2
20,000 total winning points: 20 ÷ 20,000 × $1,000 = $1

The entry’s points and the prize pool stayed fixed. Doubling the total winning points halved this illustrative share. That is why points do not carry one universal dollar value and why a displayed average should be read as an estimate.

Decision checklist before interpreting a payout

  1. Identify the entry format: is it Classic Standard/Flex or a Streak?
  2. Identify the settlement basis: if it is Champions, check the entry’s points, contest prize, and average-prize wording.
  3. For a Streak, check its stage: its first two picks, continuation toward pick 11, and any applicable void or Win Now treatment.
  4. Use the final Underdog screen: it controls the current eligibility, terms, modifiers, and grading.

Frequently asked questions

Can an Underdog Streak be settled through Champions?

Yes. A Streak describes the entry’s progression toward 11 correct picks. Underdog’s Streaks rules say that in a Champions contest, Win Now closes the Streak and locks Champions Points, so the two can operate together.

How are Underdog Champions Points calculated?

Underdog says points equal entry fee times a difficulty multiplier. Selection count, selection likelihood, and entry fee affect the points.

Why can a Champions payout differ from an average prize?

Champions is peer-to-peer. The final value depends on the prize pool and total winning Champions Points, while the average prize is an estimate based on historical win rates.

Does Propeller’s Underdog payout calculator calculate Streaks or Champions?

No. The calculator is for Classic Pick’em Standard and Flex base-table scenarios. It does not calculate Streak completion, Champions Points, or a Champions prize-pool share.

Need a Classic Standard or Flex base-table scenario?

Use the separate calculator only for Classic Pick’em Standard and Flex scenarios. It does not support Streaks or Champions calculations.
