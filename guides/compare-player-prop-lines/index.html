Keep the math honest

Sportsbook price and pick’em payout terms belong in separate columns.

Sportsbook market

American or decimal odds price one side of a market. A no-vig estimate removes the sportsbook margin to describe market-implied context—not certainty.

Pick’em or DFS entry

A multiplier applies to an entry and can depend on pick count, format, promotions, modifiers, grading, and the platform’s current rules.

Do not convert one format into the other without stating assumptions. Always keep the current platform screen as the source for its own terms.

FAQ

Rules that prevent a false comparison.

What should I match before comparing player prop lines?

Match the player, stat, direction, exact line, source, and timestamp. If one field differs, treat it as a different research question.

Are sportsbook odds and pick’em payouts directly comparable?

No. Sportsbook odds price one market outcome, while pick’em and DFS products use entry-level payout terms and rules. Record them in separate fields.

Does Propeller automatically compare every platform?

No. Propeller provides research for the displayed line. You must verify the current line, source, time, and platform terms yourself.

What if the lines do not match?

Do not force the comparison. Refresh the sources, label the mismatch, or take no action.
