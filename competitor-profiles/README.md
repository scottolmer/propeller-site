# Propeller competitor-intelligence evidence

This directory is the dated, auditable evidence package supporting the July 24, 2026 competitor analysis. It separates direct product competitors from the DFS/pick'em demand ecosystems and publisher-level organic competitors.

## Scope

- Direct products: Rithmm, Outlier, PlayerProps.ai, Props.Cash, Props Optimizer, BettingPros, OddsJam, and PropsBot.
- Demand ecosystems: PrizePicks, Underdog Fantasy, and DraftKings Pick6. They are not framed as Propeller competitors.
- Organic/AEO layer: publishers, review sites, communities, and answer-engine citation sources identified by the research.

## Evidence status labels

- **Confirmed** — a dated first-party or platform source supports the claim.
- **Observed** — a public search, archive, or storefront observation supports the claim, but not a continuous history or private metric.
- **Estimate** — a reputable third-party measurement, explicitly labeled with its source and date.
- **Inference** — an analyst conclusion based on cited facts.
- **Unavailable** — public evidence was not sufficient; no substitution or guess is made.

## Directory convention

`raw/<competitor>/<YYYY-MM-DD>/` preserves source captures by type. Synthesized profiles and the cross-market report link back to those captures and preserve source URLs plus access date. Public ad archive date spans are observations, not proof that a creative ran without interruption.
