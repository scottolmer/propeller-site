Reading a score

A score is a research signal, not a promised outcome.

The number describes the model's directional confidence for its favored side. It is not a win probability, payout estimate, or guarantee. If your submitted direction differs from the model's lean, the result says so plainly. When the available data is insufficient, you will get that explanation instead of a neutral-looking score.

Questions

What Research My Pick does and does not do.

Can I research a prop that is not on Propeller's board?

That is the point of the tool. It is designed for a supported player, upcoming event, stat, and line you bring in, including a valid custom line that does not match a current board listing.

Will every pick return a score?

No. A player, event, stat, or coverage profile may be unsupported, or current data may be insufficient. In those cases, the tool identifies the reason and does not turn the data gap into a score.

Does the final result show a payout?

No. Research My Pick does not calculate payout value or place wagers. After an event, saved picks are recorded as Win, Loss, Push, or Void based on the final stat outcome.
