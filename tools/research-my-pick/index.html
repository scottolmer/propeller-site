Reading the index

Historical support is a pattern, not a promise.

Past results are compared with your line. Recent games count more; small samples pull support toward 50. Research notes add context; Historical support is not a win probability. It is also not a payout estimate or guarantee. If your selection differs from the historical pattern, the report says so plainly.

Questions

What Research My Pick does today.

Can I research a prop that is not on Propeller's board?

Yes, for the currently enabled market: an upcoming NFL quarterback passing-yards line you enter yourself. It does not require a matching public-board listing.

Will every request return Historical support?

No. The player, event, or data can be unsupported or incomplete. In those cases, the tool identifies the reason instead of turning a gap into an index.

Does the result show a payout or place a wager?

No. Research My Pick does not calculate payout value or place wagers. Saved picks are recorded as Win, Loss, Push, or Void from the final stat outcome.
