# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Propeller Picks is primarily for casual and newer DFS and pick'em players who are evaluating a player line and want useful research without having to learn complicated sportsbook terminology. They may use PrizePicks, Underdog Fantasy, DraftKings Pick6, or another DFS/pick'em platform, but they use Propeller independently to research their own decisions.

## Product Purpose

Propeller turns player-prop research into a simpler decision workflow. It brings together relevant matchup, role, injury, recent-performance, and line context; shows a directional More/Over or Less/Under research lean; and lets the user inspect the reasons before making their own decision.

Success means a normal person can quickly understand what matters about a player line, why the research leans in a direction, and what they may want to examine next. The product should reduce research time and confusion without promising an outcome.

## Positioning

Propeller is an AI-assisted, plain-language player-prop research workspace designed specifically for use alongside DFS and pick'em platforms. Unlike a sportsbook, it does not accept wagers. Unlike tools centered on advanced betting terminology, it is intended to make the research understandable to regular people.

The founder built Propeller because he wanted a simpler way to research player props without needing to understand confusing betting jargon.

## Operating Context

People encounter a player line on a DFS or pick'em platform, use Propeller to review the research lean and supporting context, and then make their own decision outside Propeller. Propeller is available through the public website, a signed-in web workspace, iOS, and Android.

Mobile acquisition and use are a strategic priority, not a secondary companion path. The founder reports that current Google search data shows mobile activity outpacing desktop by roughly ten to one. Homepage conversion work should therefore make the relevant app-store path prominent while preserving web access for visitors who prefer it.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The signed-in product supports NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, soccer, and PGA, with availability varying by season, slate, and data.
- The public analyzer supports NBA, NHL, MLB, NFL, and soccer and is free to use without an account.
- Propeller displays a 50–100 directional model-confidence score. It is not a calibrated win probability or a guarantee.
- Only available signals contribute to a listed prop's score; signal availability and weighting vary by sport, prop, model version, and production calibration.
- Propeller supports player-line research workflows associated with PrizePicks, Underdog Fantasy, and DraftKings Pick6 but is independent of and unaffiliated with those platforms.
- Propeller does not accept wagers, place wagers, or submit DFS/pick'em entries.
- The current Founder 500 offer gives the first 500 eligible accounts free lifetime core access while spots remain. Current availability and terms shown at signup control.
- Marketing must not promise outcomes, profitability, ROI, calibrated probabilities, or unsupported product capabilities.
- Technical methodology and limitations must remain available, but the primary marketing experience should explain the product in plain English.

## Brand Commitments

- Formal name: Propeller Picks; the customer-facing shorthand “Propeller” is established.
- Preserve the existing Propeller mark and the orange brand association unless the founder explicitly approves a rebrand.
- The voice should be straightforward, human, useful, and jargon-light—not hype-driven, overly technical, or written like a sportsbook.
- The website should clearly state that Propeller was designed for DFS and pick'em research and does not accept wagers.
- iOS and Android availability should be visible and treated as a primary acquisition path.
- The homepage should use the historical dataset's scale as a prominent trust signal. The durable public wording is “2.5M+ historical prop-analysis rows graded,” with a nearby route to the exact scope and limitations. Never describe these rows as customer bets, unique props, or evidence of guaranteed accuracy.

## Product Principles

1. Explain the benefit before explaining the machinery.
2. Help regular people research a player line without requiring betting expertise.
3. Make mobile acquisition a first-class path while keeping web research available.
4. Show the reasons behind a research lean and leave the final decision to the user.
5. Earn trust through clear boundaries and inspectable evidence rather than promises.
