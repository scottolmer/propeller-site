Question-level findings

Utility and transparent methodology are earning retrieval.

Where Propeller performed best

  • Underdog flex payout calculator: 12 mentions and 11 citations across 15 completed observations.
  • PrizePicks payout calculator: 10 mentions and 8 citations.
  • Propeller vs BettingPros: 11 mentions and 6 citations across 12 observations.
  • Propeller vs OddsJam: 11 mentions, although only 2 included a Propeller citation.
  • How Propeller grades picks: 11 mentions, showing that inspectable methodology is a useful retrieval hook.

Where the category gap remains

  • ChatGPT prompt for sports betting research: 0 mentions in 12 completed observations.
  • Underdog research tool: 0 mentions and 0 citations.
  • Best AI player prop analyzer: 3 mentions but 0 citations.
  • DraftKings Pick6 research tool: 3 mentions but 0 citations.
  • Transparent research tool: 3 mentions but 0 citations, indicating a page-to-citation gap.
Interpretation: “Mentioned” and “cited” are separate outcomes. A platform may name Propeller without linking to the canonical page. The next optimization target is therefore not just more brand language; it is clearer, crawlable evidence pages that answer one intent directly.
Citation landscape

Owned pages are present, but third-party context shapes the answer.

Most-cited domains

propellerpicks.com led with 62 citations, followed by PropsBot (37), PrizePicks (30), YouTube (18), Odds Reference (15), BettingPros (9), Turtle EV Labs (9), Outlier (7), and Apple’s App Store (7).

This mix confirms that answer engines use both owned evidence and external validation. Community and publisher sources are citation opportunities, not places to manufacture mentions.

Accuracy signal

Of 102 completed rows that mentioned Propeller, 6 claims were explicitly verified against the product-fact ledger, 96 were conservatively marked unverifiable, and none were graded inaccurate or mixed.

“Unverifiable” is not the same as incorrect. It means the captured answer summary did not contain an explicit claim that the current fact ledger could resolve.

Recommended actions

Turn this snapshot into a monthly operating loop.

1. Strengthen retrieval owners

Prioritize /analyzer/, PrizePicks research, Underdog research, Pick6 research, and the transparent-methodology page. Each should contain a direct answer, current product facts, evidence links, and a clear canonical URL.

2. Close mention-to-citation gaps

The OddsJam comparison, grading, generic analyzer, Pick6 research, and transparent-research questions often mention Propeller without citing it. Tighten answer-first copy, entity references, evidence links, and canonical ownership on those pages.

3. Earn independent citations

Build evidence-led outreach around the public benchmark, payout calculators, methodology, and prospective record. Target reputable DFS/pick’em education, AI/product directories, and data-led sports or technology publishers.

4. Finish the ChatGPT cohort later

Retain the 45 failed rows honestly and retry them only after the account safeguard clears. Do not combine API web-search results with the ChatGPT Search product cohort.

Cadence established: the monthly workflow regenerates a versioned matrix, runs fixed product modes, validates row provenance and exact citation URLs, and creates the scorecard. Incomplete reports require an explicit draft flag and remain visibly non-comparable.
Method and limitations

What this snapshot does—and does not—claim.

  • The 20-question contract is frozen as version 2026-07-v1 for future verification.
  • Each observation records answer surface, brand mention, citation, exact cited URL, competitors, source type, factual accuracy, summary, and evidence URL.
  • Rates in this document use completed observations only. The 45 nonfinal ChatGPT rows are excluded from rate denominators and listed separately.
  • Google ordinary web results are not treated as AI citations. A valid run with no AI answer surface remains an absent surface.
  • Accuracy is graded conservatively against data/product-facts.json; it is not a claim about model performance or betting outcomes.
