What the data says

Search implications by competitor

Outlier is the closest search and product benchmark

Its traffic is heavily direct, which means its headline visit count is not evidence that it owns every generic SEO query. Its roughly 700 visible organic keywords and recent traffic decline make “free NFL player-prop analyzer,” “how to research NFL player props,” and transparent-methodology terms realistic mid-tail wedges.

  • Defend: free research, no black-box framing, source-aware analysis.
  • Do not claim: equivalent data breadth, line movement, or outcome superiority.

Props.Cash proves that research workflow can retain attention

Its session depth is the standout signal in the sample. The response should be to make Propeller’s research journey useful and inspectable, not to mimic its trend product or add thin comparison pages.

  • Build on: player context, research workflow, methodology, and current-slate honesty.
  • Measure: analyzer completion and repeat research sessions—not pageviews alone.

BettingPros is an authority benchmark

It has publisher reach and fantasy-sports category authority. Competing head-to-head on broad “best picks” or generic NFL content is inefficient. Take specific tool, calculator, and research questions where a direct answer can earn the click.

  • Attack: concrete how-to, payout-rule, and research-intent queries.
  • Avoid: chasing general editorial scale before earning citations and repeatable proof.

OddsJam is adjacent, not a product promise to copy

Its size and positive trend reflect an established odds/utility footprint. Propeller should use comparison and education intent to clarify its research workflow, while avoiding bids or copy that imply real-time line shopping, sportsbook integration, +EV guarantees, or automated betting.

  • Use: “OddsJam alternative” and methodology comparison only where the page is factual.
  • Exclude: optimizer, odds API, arbitrage, and guaranteed-profit language.

How to read this benchmark

  • Propeller’s first-party GA4 and Search Console snapshot: 881 sessions and 442 organic clicks in the recent 28-day windows.
  • Public Similarweb pages for the four primary comparison domains, dated June 2026.
  • Competitor role and search guardrails from the existing Propeller SEO/AEO strategy and its current AEO snapshot.

What is not claimed

  • Competitor Search Console, revenue, conversion rate, paid spend, or true organic-session counts.
  • Comparable visit figures for every competitor; public panels do not always expose them.
  • A precise Propeller-to-competitor traffic ratio: first-party GA4 sessions and third-party estimated visits are different measures and date windows.
