Position to own: Propeller is a free, inspectable AI-assisted research workspace designed to help people research DFS and pick’em player lines. It does not require confusing betting jargon, does not submit entries, and does not pretend that confidence is a guarantee.

What competitors win with

AI claims, large content libraries, platform/optimizer pages, historical filters, sportsbook tools, reviews, and app distribution.

What Propeller can own

Plain-language player-line research, free first-use value, transparent limits, concrete methodology, and DFS-first platform fit.

What not to imitate

“Best,” guaranteed outcomes, profit language, locks, sportsbook handoffs, EV/arbitrage promises, and thin sport × platform pages.

Method and evidence limits

What this report proves—and what it deliberately does not.

Evidence standards

  • Confirmed: current first-party, platform, storefront, or official-policy source.
  • Observed: public search/archive/storefront occurrence, not private account data.
  • Estimate: third-party measurement with source and date.
  • Inference: an analyst conclusion based on cited evidence.
  • Unavailable: public evidence did not support a claim.

Important limitations

  • No logins, trials, purchases, payment details, or access-control workarounds were used.
  • Public ad archive dates establish observations, not continuous runtime, spend, targeting, or performance.
  • Search results, platform rules, pricing, app ratings, and competitor claims can change.
  • The July AEO matrix is explicitly incomplete and directional only.
  • No keyword volume, CPC, traffic, ranking, citation, accuracy, or ROI value is invented.
