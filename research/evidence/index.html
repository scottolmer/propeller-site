Use with care

A short checklist for accurate citations.

Include the unit and date

Use the data source’s own date, version, and definition. Do not quote an aggregate without its raw-row, ledger, or scope qualification.

Keep confidence in scope

Directional confidence is not a calibrated probability, accuracy rate, expected value claim, or guaranteed result.

Keep the record in scope

The publication-capture ledger is not proof of pre-event ordering, outcome timing, ROI, profit, or profitability.

Prefer the primary page

Link to the source page or JSON file that owns the fact. Corrections can be requested through the editorial policy.
