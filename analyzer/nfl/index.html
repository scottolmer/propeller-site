What the NFL analyzer looks at

Role and volume
How much the offense is expected to lean on the player this week, from their recent share of the work.
Position matchup
How the opposing defense has handled the player's position, not just its overall ranking.
Injury cascades
Who is out on either side, and whose role grows or shrinks because of it.
Game environment
The expected game script and conditions that change how much a team throws or runs.
Recent form and line movement
How the player has trended lately and how the posted line has moved since it opened.

When NFL lines move

  1. MidweekPractice reports start to show who is limited. Lines open and early movement begins.
  2. FridayGame-status designations come out. Roles behind an injured starter become clearer.
  3. Game dayInactive lists post about 90 minutes before kickoff. Recheck the player, stat, line, and timestamp before you use any analysis.

Questions

Is the NFL prop analyzer free?

Yes. You can search current NFL player lines and read the research lean without an account. Public tools stay free. Account access terms are shown in the app.

Why don't I see any NFL props?

The public feed only shows current NFL lines. During the offseason, the preseason ramp, or days without listed games there may be none, and older analysis is never relabeled as current.

What does the confidence score mean?

It is a 50-100 directional model-confidence score showing how strongly the available signals support the displayed More/Over or Less/Under direction. It is not a calibrated win probability or a guarantee.

Does Propeller place bets or entries?

No. Propeller is a research tool. It does not accept wagers, place wagers, or submit DFS or pick'em entries, and it is not affiliated with PrizePicks, Underdog Fantasy, or DraftKings Pick6.
