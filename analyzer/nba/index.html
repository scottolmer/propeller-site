What the NBA analyzer looks at

Minutes and role
How long the player is expected to be on the court. Minutes multiply every other stat.
Usage after injuries
When a teammate sits, who absorbs the shots, touches, and minutes that open up.
Pace and matchup
How many possessions the game is likely to have and how the opponent defends the player's spots.
Rest and schedule
Back-to-backs and dense stretches that can trim minutes or efficiency.
Recent form and market context
Recent production, shooting context, and how the posted line has moved.

Questions

Is the NBA prop analyzer free?

Yes. You can search current NBA player lines and read the research lean without an account. Public tools stay free. Account access terms are shown in the app.

Why don't I see any NBA props?

The public feed only shows current NBA lines. On days without listed games, or when public data is unavailable, there may be none, and older analysis is never relabeled as current.

What does the confidence score mean?

It is a 50-100 directional model-confidence score showing how strongly the available signals support the displayed More/Over or Less/Under direction. It is not a calibrated win probability or a guarantee.

Does Propeller place bets or entries?

No. Propeller is a research tool. It does not accept wagers, place wagers, or submit DFS or pick'em entries, and it is not affiliated with PrizePicks, Underdog Fantasy, or DraftKings Pick6.
