How often are Propeller picks updated?

Last updated: 2026-10-13

By Scott Olmer, designated author and editorial owner. Produced and reviewed through automated AI workflows. Editorial policy.

Direct answer: Propeller's public research can change as new slates, lines, player news and available data change. Check the current research page, then recheck the exact line and any news that affects the player's participation or role. A page dated today does not establish when every underlying input was captured. Settled outcomes belong in Results.

Which timestamp answers your question?

Check what each date refers to. An article date, page heading or response-generation time does not tell you when the line was observed or the analysis was made. Look for those specific times and record Unknown when they are missing.

Documentation date: when the article was marked updated. Use it to check the explanation’s revision history, not the age of the research inputs.

Response-generation time: when that response was produced. Its line and analysis may be older; this timestamp does not supply their ages.

How old are the inputs? Seek the line-observation and analysis times for the exact research question. Match the player, event, stat, number and direction before treating a timestamp as relevant to your note.

What should I recheck when something changes?

Recheck your research when player status, expected role, the posted line or the slate changes. The checklist below tells you what to look for; it does not promise that Propeller updates automatically after each change.

Can I still use research I read this morning?

Reopen the current research and check whether the information you relied on still applies. For a hypothetical example, suppose you read a note at 10 AM and player news arrives at noon. The earlier note cannot certify that the noon change has been incorporated. Look for research that addresses the changed participation or role before relying on that assumption.

A changed number also deserves a fresh look. Match the player, event, stat, number and direction to the exact line you are evaluating. Research about another number does not automatically answer the same question. If you cannot match them, wait.

Why might no current research appear?

If no current research appears, check the selected sport and the page's availability message. An empty public view alone does not tell you whether no rows were returned, the feed could not be reached, or another publishing constraint applies. Wait for usable current research rather than treating an older slate as current.

Coverage also varies with sport, season, slate and available data. Use the supported-sports help page to understand coverage, then inspect the current page for availability. A general coverage list cannot confirm that a particular player or event has current research.

How should I read an unknown timestamp?

Record missing line-observation or analysis times as Unknown. A missing timestamp does not show whether an input is old, failed or zero, and a fresh page heading cannot supply it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does today's date mean every source is current?

No. A page or slate date does not establish when its underlying line or analysis was captured. Read any available timestamp in context: what was timed, for which player and event, and whether it answers your freshness question.

Where do settled outcomes appear?

Use Results for settled outcomes. Current research answers what is available to inspect before an event; settled results answer what happened afterward. If an outcome remains ungraded or unresolved, wait for its status rather than counting it yourself as settled.
